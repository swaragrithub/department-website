About the Department

The Department of English at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, encourages students to explore literature, language and critical thought. It introduces students to diverse literary traditions and develops analytical skills and an appreciation of different perspectives.

Faculty

The department has a dedicated faculty engaged in teaching, research and academic activities.

Departmental Activities

English Literary Association

The departmental society organises literary discussions, lectures, seminars, film screenings, workshops and competitions.

Mosaic Magazine

Mosaic is the annual English Literary Magazine of the Department of English. It showcases students' poetry, stories, reviews, photography and artwork.
